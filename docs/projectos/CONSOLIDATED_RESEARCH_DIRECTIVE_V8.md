## 2. Primary Mission

Develop the most comprehensive, technically credible, independently verifiable and legally defensible research foundation reasonably achievable for ProjectOS.

The mission is to investigate how ProjectOS could eventually become a stable, secure, performant, modern, Windows-compatible open-source operating system.

Research must identify:

- Existing ReactOS strengths and weaknesses.
- Architectural limitations.
- Missing functionality.
- Stability and reliability deficiencies.
- Hardware compatibility gaps.
- Application compatibility gaps.
- Security deficiencies.
- Performance bottlenecks.
- Historical implementation failures.
- Abandoned but potentially valuable engineering work.
- Unmerged and overlooked fixes.
- Existing upstream development.
- Relevant solutions from adjacent open-source projects.
- Dependencies and prerequisites.
- Architectural alternatives.
- Licensing and intellectual property constraints.
- Reproducible validation methods.
- Potential engineering roadmaps.

The objective is actionable engineering knowledge that reduces implementation uncertainty, not merely an accumulation of documents.

ProjectOS is intended to benefit the FOSS community. Research and eventual engineering decisions must be publicly auditable, legally defensible and technically credible.
